# Changelog

All notable changes to image are recorded here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `image.PixelFormat`, twenty interleaved formats from `gray8` to `rgba_f32_premul`, and `image.PixelBuffer`, the caller's pixel memory with a stride.
- `image.convert`: conversion and swizzle between any two pixel formats, with SIMD for the common pairs; `convert.Converter` picks the kernel once for many rows.
- `image.PixelFormat.cmyk8` and `cmyk16`: CMYK stored inverted, as Adobe's JPEGs store it (the maximum is no ink); `image.convert` turns them into color by libjpeg-turbo's naive conversion (SIMD from `cmyk8`) and color into them by its inverse.
- `image.PixelFormat.rgb_f32` and `gray_f32`, float formats beside `rgba_f32`; `image.convert` converts them to and from every format, with SIMD between float and 8- or 16-bit samples in the same layout (5–16× the general path for float to bytes).
- `image.Metadata.primaries` (red, green, blue, and white xy, in place of the removed `Metadata.chromaticities` and `image.Chromaticities`; PNG's cHRM fills it, and `png.EncodeOptions.primaries` replaces `chromaticities` to write it), `Metadata.intensity_target`, `Metadata.jpeg_reconstruction` (the JPEG XL file carries a `jbrd` box), and `image.Transfer.bt709`, `dci`, `pq`, and `hlg`; a plain gamma stays in `Metadata.gamma`.
- `image.Config`, `image.Frame`, `image.Limits`, `image.DecodeOptions`, `image.Metadata`, and `image.DecodeError`, shared by every decoder; `image.exif.orientation` reads EXIF orientation.
- `image.detect` names a file's `image.Format` from its first `image.detect_prefix_len` bytes, for every phase-1 format.
- `image.Decoder` decodes any supported format behind one interface; `Decoder.initDetect` picks the format from the input.
- `image.BlobSink` takes blobs whose length is known only at their end: `image.Blob.len` is `?u64`, null until the last piece, and `Blob.endsAt` says whether a piece ends its blob.
- `image.Decoder.decodeFrameConfig` returns the next frame before `decodeFrame` draws it, for every format: GIF, ICO, PNG and APNG, and Netpbm read it ahead themselves, and one-frame formats report the canvas.
- `image.Decoder.finalConfig` returns the `Config` updated with metadata a format finds after its frames, once `decodeFrame` has returned null.
- `image.qoi.Decoder`, a streaming QOI decoder that writes any pixel format and needs no work memory.
- `image.bmp` and `image.ico`: BMP decode (every header, 1- to 32-bit, RLE, bitfields; embedded JPEG and PNG reported as `error.EmbeddedJpeg` and `error.EmbeddedPng`) and encode (24-bit, 32-bit with alpha, 8-bit indexed); ICO and CUR decode of every entry with hotspots, out-of-order entries through `DecodeOptions.seeker`, and encode of BMP entries.
- `image.tga`: `Decoder` reads every TGA image type (color-mapped, true-color, and gray, run-length encoded or not, any origin) and reports the TGA 2.0 footer and extension area through `Decoder.trailer`; `encode`, `workLen`, and `EncodeOptions` write uncompressed or run-length encoded files.
- `image.netpbm`: PBM, PGM, and PPM (plain and raw), PAM, and PFM; `netpbm.Decoder` streams any pixel format with no work memory and reads a raw file's concatenated images as frames, and `netpbm.encode` writes every kind (`netpbm.Kind`, with `EncodeOptions.maxval` and the PFM byte order).
- `image.ico.Decoder.decodeFrameConfig` reads the next entry's header and returns its frame before it is drawn.
- `image.ico.Decoder` decodes PNG entries picked with `Options.entry` through `image.png`.
- `image.ico.encode` writes 256×256 images as PNG entries (`ico.Payload.auto`, the default; `.bmp` and `.png` pick one), so `ico.workLen` is `png.workLen` for them; `ico.encodeAll` takes a `work` argument and `Image.payload`; `ico.Decoder` decodes a file's only entry when it is a PNG without `Options.entry`.
- `image.ico.Decoder` decodes PNG entries among several entries without `Options.entry`, on the canvas; a file with several entries now reports `Config.work_len` of `png.Decoder.maxWorkLen` for the canvas width (about 76 KiB at 256), which `decodeFrame` requires even when every entry is a DIB.
- `image.png.EncodeOptions.max_depth` (`png.MaxDepth`): `.@"8"` stores 16-bit and float sources at 8 bits a sample, still reduced, with no more work memory; `image.ico.encode` uses it, so its PNG entries are never 16-bit.
- `image.png.Decoder`, a streaming PNG and APNG decoder: every color type and bit depth, Adam7, tRNS, APNG frames with their region, disposal, and blend, and gAMA, cHRM, sRGB, pHYs, bKGD, iCCP, and eXIf reported; CRCs of critical chunks and the Adler-32 are checked, image data must fill exactly the frame (except after the last row of an interlaced image one row tall, which is skipped, as in Wuffs), and it needs about 72 KiB plus two rows of work memory.
- `image.tga.Decoder.applyTrailer` and `image.png.Decoder.applyTrailer`: `image.Decoder.finalConfig` reports TGA's extension-area gamma, aspect ratio, and key color, and a PNG eXIf that follows the image data with its orientation.
- `image.png.Decoder` checks `Limits.max_frames` as frames are reached, not against acTL's count: a file whose acTL claims too many frames decodes the frames within the limit, with `Config.frame_count` null, and fails with `error.TooManyFrames` at the frame past it; gAMA, cHRM, sRGB, pHYs, bKGD, and iCCP after the image data are ignored, as the specification puts them before it.
- `image.png.Decoder.Options.ignore_checksums` skips the critical chunks' CRCs and the Adler-32, as Wuffs's `IGNORE_CHECKSUM` quirk does; off by default.
- `image.jpeg.Decoder`, a streaming JPEG decoder whose pixels match libjpeg-turbo's defaults bit for bit: baseline, extended, and progressive DCT images with 8- or 12-bit samples, Huffman- or arithmetic-coded, lossless images (process 14) of 2 to 16 bits (`gray16`, `rgb16`, and `cmyk16` native past 8 bits), restart intervals, every sampling factor, grayscale, YCbCr, RGB, CMYK, and YCCK (to RGB by default, or to their inks as native `cmyk8` with `Options.cmyk = .raw`), decoding at 1/2, 1/4, or 1/8 size (`Options.scale`), TIFF's JPEGTables (`Options.tables`, `Options.color`), edge tiles (`Options.crop`, a destination smaller than the frame), and work memory from a container's fields before the stream (`Decoder.workLen`), and JFIF density, EXIF orientation, and ICC (reassembled from its APP2 segments), EXIF, and XMP reported; lossless arithmetic coding and the hierarchical processes return `error.Unsupported`, as libjpeg-turbo does not decode them either.
- Decoders pull from a `std.Io.Reader` whose buffer holds at least `image.min_buffer_len` (64) bytes, and read it forward; a format whose offsets can point backward (ICO) moves back only through the `image.Seeker` the caller passes in `DecodeOptions.seeker`, which `image.Decoder` passes through, and fails without one.
- `image.gif.Decoder`, a streaming GIF87a and GIF89a decoder: interlacing, transparency, local and global color tables, animation frames with their region, disposal (restore to previous included), and blend, delays in flicks, and the NETSCAPE2.0 loop count; `Decoder.decodeFrameConfig` reports the next frame before it is drawn, and it needs about 68 KiB of work memory.
- `image.gif.Decoder` reports `Config.loop_count` as 1, not null, for a file without a loop extension, as browsers play it once.
- `image.gif.Decoder.Options.ignore_too_much_data` accepts a frame whose LZW data holds more pixels than the frame, dropping them, as Wuffs's IGNORE_TOO_MUCH_PIXEL_DATA quirk does; off by default, when such a frame is `error.TooMuchData`.
- `image.gif.Decoder` reports ICC profiles and XMP packets to `DecodeOptions.blob_sink` and `Config.metadata`, and ones after the first frame through `image.Decoder.finalConfig`.
- `image.gif.encode` and `image.gif.encodeAnimation`, with `workLen`, `EncodeOptions`, `EncodeError`, and `AnimationFrame`: GIF89a encode from any pixel format. An image or frame with at most 256 colors (alpha below 128 counting as one, transparent) is stored exactly; one with more is quantized (`Quantizer.wu` by default, or `.median_cut` and `.octree`, with `EncodeOptions.refine` k-means passes) and dithered (`Dither.floyd_steinberg` by default, or `.none`). Animations have per-frame delays and disposal, the NETSCAPE2.0 loop count (`EncodeOptions.loop_count`), and frames cropped to what changes (`EncodeOptions.crop`); `EncodeOptions.interlace` interlaces. Work memory is about 2.4 MiB for an image of more than 256 pixels (plus a byte a pixel to interlace a dithered one), and 6 KiB otherwise.
- `image.webp.Decoder`, a streaming WebP decoder for lossless (VP8L) and lossy (VP8) files, simple and extended, with ALPH alpha (raw or lossless, every filter) and ICCP, EXIF, and XMP reported; lossy pixels match libwebp's default output exactly, and `Options.max_prefix_groups` (default 2600) bounds its work memory; animations decode frame by frame into their regions of the caller's canvas, blended as libwebp blends them, with `Config.loop_count` and `Config.background` from ANIM and `Decoder.decodeFrameConfig` for the next frame; ICCP, EXIF, and XMP chunks after the image reach `image.Decoder.finalConfig`.
- `image.webp.Decoder.Options.seeker`: an animation frame whose ALPH chunk is another size than its ANMF chunk says decodes at its VP8 frame's size, as libwebp decodes it; without a seeker such a frame fails with `error.AlphaBehind`.
- `image.tiff.Decoder`, a streaming TIFF and BigTIFF decoder: strips and tiles, chunky and planar; uncompressed, PackBits, LZW, Deflate, CCITT modified Huffman, Group 3, and Group 4, and JPEG data (new style, 8- or 12-bit, through `image.jpeg`, with JPEGTables and the tables one strip's stream defines kept for the next, as libjpeg keeps them, and gray with alpha as two components); predictors 2 and 3; 1- to 32-bit integer and 16- to 64-bit float samples; gray, palette, RGB, CMYK (its inks as `cmyk8` or `cmyk16`, which `image.convert` turns into color), and subsampled YCbCr; every page a frame with its own size, which `Decoder.decodeFrameConfig` reports before the page is decoded. IFDs and data behind the reader need `DecodeOptions.seeker` (`error.DataBehind` without one); progressive or non-interleaved JPEG streams in TIFF are `error.Unsupported`.
- `image.webp.lossy.encode`, with `workLen`, `EncodeOptions`, `EncodeError`, and `Effort`: a lossy WebP (VP8) encoder from any pixel format, `EncodeOptions.quality` 0 to 100 (default 75) on cwebp's scale, three efforts (`.fast`, `.default`, `.best`), four segments with their own quantizer and loop filter level, alpha in an ALPH chunk compressed by the lossless encoder when that is smaller (`EncodeOptions.alpha_compression`, on by default; off leaves the lossless encoder's work memory out of `workLen`), colors under fully transparent pixels treated as free, and ICCP, EXIF, and XMP in a VP8X file; `error.EmptyImage` for an image without pixels, and `error.ImageTooLarge` past 16383 pixels a side.
- `image.png.encode` and `image.png.encodeAnimation`, with `workLen`, `EncodeOptions`, and `EncodeError`: PNG and APNG encode that writes the smallest lossless color type and bit depth (palette, gray, bit depths from 1 to 16, tRNS), chooses each row's filter, has three efforts (`Effort.fast`, `.default`, `.best`), writes APNG frames with region, disposal, and blend, and writes gAMA, cHRM, sRGB, iCCP, pHYs (`EncodeOptions.density`), and eXIf; work memory is about 831 KiB plus 72 bytes per pixel of width.
- `image.tiff.encode` and `image.tiff.encodePages`: classic little-endian TIFF, one page or several, uncompressed or PackBits, LZW (the default), or Deflate (`EncodeOptions.compression`, `.effort`), Predictor 2, strips of about 8 KiB (`.rows_per_strip`), gray, gray and alpha, RGB, RGBA (straight or associated), and CMYK at 8 or 16 bits, a palette when it is smaller (`.palette`), and resolution (`.density`); files read forward, without a seeker, with uncompressed, PackBits, and LZW strips byte for byte as libtiff writes them; `tiff.workLen` gives the work memory.
- `image.EncodeError`, the errors every encoder shares; each format's encoder will be `encode(writer, pixels, options, work)` with `workLen` for its work memory.
- `image.jxl.Decoder`, a streaming JPEG XL decoder: VarDCT and Modular frames, XYB and YCbCr, progressive passes, the restoration filters, upsampling, patches, splines, noise, animation with blending and reference frames, extra channels, bare codestreams and containers, and the ICC profile, `Exif`, and `xml ` boxes reported; `rgba_f32` and `rgba_f32_premul` destinations are written from its floats, without a 16-bit step (clamped to [0, 1] for integer images).
- `image.jxl.Decoder.decodeFrame` returns the last frame once its codestream is complete and reads the container boxes after it on the next call, so a broken or cut-off box there fails that call (with the image already returned) instead of losing the image.
- `image.jxl.Decoder` reports `Metadata.primaries`, `Metadata.intensity_target`, and the BT.709, DCI, PQ, and HLG transfer functions from the colour encoding; for an XYB image with an ICC profile, linear sRGB, the space its pixels are written in (the profile describes the original).
- `image.jxl.Decoder` decodes a large single-frame VarDCT image (five group rows or more, without patches, splines, noise, upsampling, or chroma subsampling) a group row at a time into the caller's buffer: peak work memory on a 50-megapixel photograph falls from 627 MB to 127 MB, and `Config.work_len` with it when the image has no alpha.
- `image.jxl.Decoder.decodeJpeg` and `jpegWorkLen` read a recompressed JPEG's quantization tables, sampling, and quantized coefficients (`Decoder.Jpeg`) without decoding pixels, or fail with `error.NotJpeg` (also when the DCT8 table is raw over a denominator other than a JPEG's 1/(8·255), which libjxl will not rebuild either); they are what JPEG reconstruction from `jbrd` builds on.
- `image.jxl.JpegReconstructor` rebuilds, byte for byte, the JPEG a JPEG XL file was transcoded from (`cjxl --lossless_jpeg=1`), from its `jbrd` box and coefficients with no pixels: `init`, `workLen`, and `run(writer, work)`; it needs `DecodeOptions.seeker` (`error.NeedsSeeker`), and a file without the box is `error.NoJbrd`.
- The package depends on `compress` at commit 61998af (after 0.2.0, for its faster inflate, its lazy deflate levels, and its writer's block scratch in the caller's buffer), which brings `fastmem`.
- `image.jxl.encode`, `workLen`, `EncodeOptions`, and `Effort` (`fast`, `default`, `best`): lossless JPEG XL (Modular) encoding of any pixel format to a bare codestream, at 8 bits for 8-bit sources and 16 otherwise (CMYK as RGB), with a palette or color transform and a learned tree, decoding bit for bit in libjxl and jxl-oxide.
- `image.wbmp`: WBMP type 0, a streaming `wbmp.Decoder` that writes any pixel format, and `wbmp.encode`, which stores gray of at least 128 as white.
- `image.qoi.encode`, `qoi.workLen`, and `qoi.EncodeOptions`: a QOI encoder that writes the same bytes as the reference encoder (qoi.h) and needs no work memory.
- `image.jpeg.Decoder.frameParams` returns the frame header after `decodeConfig`, `Decoder.keptTables` and `Options.prior` carry a stream's quantization and Huffman tables into the next as libjpeg does across TIFF strips, `Options.tables` reads a tables stream as libjpeg reads a header (SOFn checked and dropped, DNL, RSTn, and TEM passed over), and two-component streams decode to native `gray_alpha8` or `gray_alpha16`.
- `image.jpeg.encode`, `jpeg.workLen`, `jpeg.EncodeOptions`, and `jpeg.Subsampling`: a JPEG encoder that writes libjpeg-turbo's bytes for the same settings: baseline or progressive, 4:4:4, 4:2:2, or 4:2:0 (one component for gray sources), standard or optimized Huffman tables, libjpeg's quality scaling, JFIF with EXIF and ICC written when given (`error.MetadataTooLarge` past what the segments hold), from any pixel format with alpha dropped; a baseline file with standard tables streams an MCU row at a time.
- `image.webp.encode`, `webp.workLen`, and `webp.EncodeOptions`: a lossless WebP (VP8L) encoder with the subtract-green, predictor, color, and color-indexing transforms, LZ77 with a color cache, and prefix codes per tile, at `effort` 0 to 9 like cwebp's `-z` (default 5); ICC, EXIF, and XMP go in a VP8X file. `webp.encodeAlpha` writes an alpha plane as the lossless stream of an ALPH chunk.
- `image.Metadata.cicp` (`Metadata.Cicp`, ITU-T H.273 code points), `Metadata.mastering_display` (`Metadata.MasteringDisplay`, xy and nits), and `Metadata.content_light_level` (`Metadata.ContentLightLevel`, MaxCLL and MaxFALL in nits): `image.png.Decoder` reads PNG's cICP, mDCV, and cLLI, and a cICP now decides `Metadata.transfer`, `gamma`, and `primaries` over iCCP, sRGB, gAMA, and cHRM (null for code points they cannot name), and the first of each chunk with the right length decides even when its values are invalid, as in libpng; `image.png.EncodeOptions.cicp`, `mastering_display`, and `content_light_level` write them.
- `image.Metadata.gain_map`: `image.jpeg.Decoder` flags a JPEG that carries an HDR gain map (Ultra HDR, Adobe's gain map, or ISO 21496-1); the pixels are still the base image's, and the gain map is not decoded.

### Changed

- Faster where large buffers are cleared, since Zig 0.16 links compiler_rt's byte-at-a-time `memset` even with libc: BMP RLE decode 1.57× and ICO decode 1.48× (geomean), JPEG decode 1.14× (progressive 1.26×), JPEG in TIFF 1.21×, TIFF LZW encode 1.11× (2.2× on 16×16), and GIF and WebP lossless encode 1.05–1.15×.
- `image.jxl.encode` writes smaller files at `.fast` (2.7% on the benchmark corpus) and `.default` (0.9%, cjxl e7's size) at about 0.8× the speed above icon size, encodes icons 1.2–1.7× faster, and touches far less memory up to a megapixel (peak over the corpus 158 to 124 MiB at `.default`, 255 to 144 MiB at `.best`).
- `image.jxl.encode` at `.default` encodes about 10% faster above icon size, for files 0.15% larger on the benchmark corpus: its tree stops at 1024 leaves.
- `image.jxl.encode` gives each 256×256 group of a larger image its own palette or color transform: screenshots and other mixed images come out 3–10% smaller at `.default` and `.best`, and the benchmark corpus 0.8%, 1.4%, and 1.5% smaller at the three efforts.
- `image.jxl.encode` encodes the same files faster: icons 1.3–1.5×, images up to a megapixel 1.06×, 1.22×, and 1.39× at `.fast`, `.default`, and `.best`.
- `image.jxl.workLen` asks for far less work memory for small images: at 16×16, 0.5, 0.8, and 3.8 MiB at `.fast`, `.default`, and `.best` (was 3.8, 5.6, and 8.1), and at 64×64 2.5, 3.4, and 6.8 MiB (was 5.4, 8.8, and 11.8).
- `image.jxl.encode` at `.default` learns from more of an image above a megapixel (one sample in 16, up to 2^20): such images come out 1.0% smaller on the benchmark corpus (screenshots 1.2–2.3%), for about 9% more time on them and up to 25 MB more `workLen` (peak over the corpus 126 to 142 MiB); smaller images are unchanged.
- `image.png.Decoder` decodes an APNG whose default image's fcTL gives a region smaller than the canvas as a frame of the whole canvas, as libpng and libspng show it, instead of failing with `error.BadChunk`.
- `image.webp.encodeAlpha` writes alpha planes 5–9% smaller and faster at every effort (at effort 1, 8.8% smaller in 18% less time), and effort 9 is now its smallest; the output is a different, still lossless stream.
- `image.gif.encode` quantizes small images faster with `Quantizer.wu`: 1.16× on 32×32 and 64×64 icons and icon animations, with the same palettes.
- `image.gif.Dither.floyd_steinberg` passes on no error from a pixel within 1 a channel of its palette entry: 1.3% smaller files on photos and screenshots (up to 14% on graphics), with PSNR +0.36 dB and SSIM +0.003.
- `image.gif.encode` stores an image of at most 256 colors 1.14–1.25× faster, reading its pixels once instead of twice up to about 2.4 megapixels, with the same bytes and work memory.
- `image.webp.encode` is faster, up to 1.19× on icons and 1.15× to 1 MP, with the same output except at efforts 1 and 2, which stop searching for matches at 64 pixels (0.1% larger, up to 5% on flat images); `webp.workLen` asks for 0.17–0.31 MB on a 16×16 icon, from 0.52–4.1 MB.
- `image.webp.encode` is 1.28× faster over the benchmark rows with the same output, 1.17–2.66× on 16 KP–1 MP images at efforts 1 to 9 and up to 2.5× on flat images at effort 5; `webp.encodeAlpha` is 1.28×, 1.79×, and 2.71× faster at efforts 1, 5, and 8; `webp.workLen` asks for up to 36 KiB less at efforts 1 to 7 and up to 228 KiB more at 8 and 9.
- `image.jpeg.encode` is faster: baseline color over 1 MP from 0.93× to 1.18× libjpeg-turbo's speed (4:4:4 from 0.98× to 1.26×), and 2.16× over all encode benchmark rows, with the same bytes.
- `image.convert` runs a row's last pixels through its SIMD kernels instead of the per-pixel path, and RGB↔BGR stores whole cache lines: narrow rows convert up to twice as fast, so 24- and 32-bit BMP and TGA decodes of narrow images gain up to 25%; `convert.Converter.run` may convert in place between formats of one pixel size.
- `image.png.Decoder` computes the chunks' CRC-32 with carry-less multiplication on x86-64 CPUs that have PCLMULQDQ, about 4% of a decode.
- `image.tiff.encode` writes Deflate strips 1.04–1.05× faster and `image.png.encode` icons 1.03–1.04×, from `compress` 61998af, which clears its encoder state without compiler_rt's byte-wise `memset`.
- `image.tiff.Decoder` decodes Deflate photographs 1.02–1.05× faster and `image.png.Decoder` images past icons 1.03×, from `compress` e383aa8's inflate loop.
- `image.tiff.Decoder` decodes Deflate data 1.11–1.19× faster (1.02–1.12× libtiff's speed past icons) and `image.png.Decoder` decodes icons 1.15–1.2× faster and larger images 1.03×, from `compress` 364ef40's inflate, which builds a block's Huffman tables and reads its header faster.
- `image.tiff.Decoder` decodes LZW data about 1.8× faster past icons (at libtiff's speed), Deflate 1.3–1.4×, PackBits 3–3.4×, and Group 4 2.6–3.2×, and icons of every compression 1.3–3× faster; an LZW page needs 24 KiB more work memory (`Config.work_len`).
- `image.png.Decoder` unfilters consecutive Average or Paeth rows two at a time, with no more work memory: up to 1.28× faster on Paeth-filtered images such as the 50-megapixel test photo.
- `image.jxl.Decoder` decodes lossy JPEG XL about 1.3× faster (up to 1.8× on large images, 1.04× on 16×16 icons) and lossless 1.16× faster, and `image.jxl.JpegReconstructor` rebuilds JPEGs 1.35–1.54× faster (up to 2× past icons), with no more memory; lossy pixels can differ by one 16-bit step in under 0.02% of samples, and `Decoder.jpegWorkLen` asks for less (9.2 MB on a 16×16 file, from 10.0; 25 MB on a 768×512 photo, from 37).
- `image.jxl.Decoder` decodes lossy JPEG XL about 1.14× faster again (1.2–1.3× on 16×16 and 64×64 icons, 1.04–1.06× on large photos), and `image.jxl.JpegReconstructor` 1.02× faster, with the same pixels and memory.
- `image.jxl.Decoder` decodes lossy JPEG XL about 1.06× faster again (1.08–1.13× on photos) and lossless 1.09× faster, and `image.jxl.JpegReconstructor` 1.03× faster, with the same pixels and memory; `Config.work_len` grows by 393 KB.
- `image.png.Decoder` expands 8-bit palette images up to 1.23× faster.
- `image.qoi.encode` encodes packed `rgba8` and `rgb8` sources as one span instead of row by row, and checks one bound per pixel instead of two: about 1.16× faster on icons and images up to 1 MP, up to 1.7× on screenshots of runs and INDEX chunks.
- `image.qoi.Decoder` checks one count per chunk instead of two bounds: about 1.03× faster, up to 1.16× on run- and INDEX-heavy images.
- `image.qoi.Decoder` decodes images of every size with one loop and takes RGBA chunks on their own branch: a stream of distinct icons decodes 1.07–1.75× faster and other images up to 1.28×, while one 16×16 or 32×32 icon decoded again and again is up to 1.5× slower.
- `image.qoi.Decoder` decodes about 1.2× faster, up to 2.2× on screenshots made mostly of INDEX chunks and runs.
- `image.webp.Decoder` decodes lossless (VP8L) files about 1.24× faster (1.37× on icons, up to 2.1× on palette screenshots with long runs), by adding green back and copying backward references in SIMD blocks, building prefix tables without scanning unused lengths, and specializing the pixel loop; no more work memory.
- `image.webp.lossy.encode` writes smaller files: trellis quantization at `.default` and `.best` (1.6% and 2.5% smaller at equal PSNR, 4–9% slower at `.default` above icon sizes and 16% on icons, `.best` 1.6× slower), and alpha compressed at the lossless encoder's efforts 1, 1, and 8 (3.6% smaller at `.fast` on images with transparency); `.fast` runs about 1.35× faster for the same files; with `alpha_compression`, `workLen` is about 29 bytes a pixel at `.fast` and `.default` and 38 at `.best`.
- `image.webp.lossy.encode` is faster on small images, for the same files at `.fast` and `.default`: a 16×16 icon takes about 0.45× the time at `.default` and 0.3× at `.fast` (icons up to 64×64 1.5× and 2.1× faster on average); `.best` is 1.3–1.8× faster, its first pass now searching as `.default` does, for files the same size.
- `image.webp.Decoder` decodes lossy (VP8) files about 1.25× faster (1.31× on icons, 1.20× over 1 MP), with a faster boolean decoder and YUV-to-RGB conversion in 16-bit lanes that keeps the CPU out of 512-bit vectors; pixels are unchanged.
- `image.netpbm.Decoder` reads plain (ASCII) PBM, PGM, and PPM samples about 1.75× faster, and headers about 1.4× faster, which shows on 16×16 files.
